The whole asset life cycle

From purchase order to write-off

Modules are role-gated — each person only sees what they're allowed to.

📇

Asset Register

One asset, one permanent number, one record. A tabbed profile with financials, the custody chain, movement history, its tag and a field-level audit trail of every change and why.

🧾

Acquisition & Capitalization

Build the capitalized cost line by line — price, freight, duties, installation, testing — then capitalize into one or many assets. Construction-in-progress projects accrue until they're commissioned.

🏷️

Tagging & Identification

Issue QR, barcode or RFID tags, print a tag sheet, mark them affixed. A scan console identifies any asset — who holds it, where it should be — and flags a mismatch.

👤

Custody & Assignment

Every asset is someone's responsibility. Capture the acknowledgement and condition at hand-over, process returns and transfers, and authorise home-use and off-site permits.

🗺️

Location & Movement

A site → building → floor → room tree. Moving an asset is request → approve → execute → receive, with an immutable history. Idle assets surface on their own.

📉

Depreciation Engine

Straight-line, declining balance, double-declining, sum-of-years and units-of-production. Book and tax schedules in parallel. Each month is calculated, validated and posted — then locked.

🔧

Repairs & Betterments

Log every repair with its cost. Routine work is an expense that builds the upkeep history; a betterment is capitalised onto the asset and lifts depreciation over the remaining life.

✅

Physical Verification

Run a count against the register — found, moved, needs repair or missing, each with a cause and a sign-off. Closing the count stamps "last verified" and routes the exceptions.

📊

Reports & Dashboard

A month-by-month depreciation schedule with actuals and projections, a forecast to zero, the fixed-asset register, and a dashboard alert centre that watches everything that's slipping.

Depreciation

Depreciation you can defend, month after month

  • Five methods — straight-line to units-of-production — per asset or from the class default
  • Book and tax runs in parallel; the run detail shows the deferred-tax difference
  • Each monthly run is calculated → validated → posted, then permanently locked
  • The Depreciation Schedule lays out every asset's whole year — posted months as actuals, the rest projected
Verification

Where the register meets reality

  • Count the whole register, or one location, class, custodian or department
  • The expected list is snapshotted; counters mark each asset OK, wrong-location, needs-repair or missing
  • Every variance closes with a cause code and a sign-off
  • "Needs repair" flips the asset to under-repair; "missing" enters the loss protocol
Custody & Tagging

Every asset accounted for — to a person

  • Assign to an employee or a department, with acknowledgement and condition notes
  • The employee asset ledger is the separation-clearance list
  • QR / barcode / RFID tags, a printable tag sheet and a scan console
  • Home-use and off-site permits with a return date and an overdue list

Questions

Good to know

Is it a real fixed-asset system, or a spreadsheet with a UI?

It runs a proper depreciation engine — five methods, book and tax bases, locked monthly runs — a full acquisition-to-disposal life cycle, tagging, custody, movement control and physical verification. Every field change is stamped with the user, the time and the reason.

Does it handle book and tax depreciation?

Yes. Each period you post a book run and a tax run in parallel; the run detail shows the deferred-tax difference, and the schedule and forecast can be viewed on either basis. The fiscal-year start month is a setting.

Can we import our existing register?

Yes. Download the Excel template, paste your assets into it — including opening accumulated depreciation for assets already in service — and the import checks every row and shows you the result before anything is saved.

Can several people use it at the same time?

Yes. Staff PCs connect to the Host over your office network — the desktop app or a browser. One server process owns the single database and runs every query in order, so there is never a write conflict. A team of dozens is well within range.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs on your own network. The only time the internet matters is confirming the Microsoft Store licence occasionally; after that it runs offline for months at a time.

Where is my data stored?

In one SQLite database file on the Host machine's disk, plus an uploads folder for photos and documents. It is never sent anywhere. You can back it up, restore it, or move it to another PC from inside the app.

How is it licensed?

It's a paid Microsoft Store app with a 30-day free trial. The licence is tied to your Microsoft account; a copied install won't run on another machine. One purchase covers one server — staff PCs connect to it and need nothing of their own.

Can we brand it with our company?

Yes. Upload your logo, set your company details and pick theme colours — it flows through the app and the printed reports.
