In short

  • The app runs on your school's own computer. Every learner, guardian and staff record lives in a database file on that computer.
  • RamRam Dev, the developer, never receives, sees or stores your school's records. There is no cloud account and no analytics.
  • Data leaves your school only if you switch on something that sends it: an SMS gateway, an email server, or an external system link.
  • The Microsoft Store checks your licence. That check involves your Microsoft account, not your school's records.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how RRD Suite: School Management System (the "app") handles personal information. The app is published on the Microsoft Store by RamRam Dev ("we", "us"). It applies to:

  • schools and institutions that install the app;
  • the people who use it — administrators, registrar, teachers, cashiers and other staff;
  • the people it holds records about — learners, applicants, parents and guardians, employees and alumni — including those who sign in to the parent, student, faculty or alumni portals.

2. Who is responsible for the data

The school that installs the app decides what information to collect and why. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), the school is the personal information controller.

The app is software the school runs on its own equipment. Because school records never reach us, RamRam Dev does not process them and is not the school's personal information processor. Questions about a specific learner's or employee's records should go to the school's Data Protection Officer or registrar.

If you are a parent, student or staff member, the school — not RamRam Dev — can view, correct or delete your records.

4. Where the data is kept

All of the above is stored in a single database file, rrd_school_management.db, plus a folder of uploaded files. Both are on the computer where the school installed the app (the "Host"). By default they are in the Windows user's application-data folder, RRDSchoolManagement.

  • Staff computers in Client mode, and browsers when web access is turned on, connect to the Host over the school's own network. They read from the Host and do not keep their own copy of the database.
  • Backups are files the app writes to the Host: a nightly backup with a weekly full copy, plus any backup the school downloads. The school decides where those files go after that.
  • The browser keeps small settings such as the selected workspace, plus the sign-in session. It does not keep school records.

6. What the developer receives

Nothing from your school's records. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or usage tracking. The "Server Status" page shows who is connected right now, but that information stays in the Host's memory and is cleared when the app restarts.

The only outside service the app contacts on its own is the Microsoft Store licensing service, to confirm the app is licensed or in trial. That check is handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement, using the Microsoft account that bought the app. To stop a copied install from running elsewhere, the app keeps a fingerprint of the licensed computer together with the licence receipt. Both stay on that computer.

If you write to us for support, we see only what you choose to send. Please don't include learner records in support messages; describe the problem or use the sample school instead.

7. Portals and children's data

Many learners are minors, so the app limits who can see their records:

  • A parent's portal opens only after the registrar verifies the link to each child. Parents see only their linked children.
  • If a court order restricts a guardian's access, that child stays hidden from them — even by a direct link.
  • Students see only their own record. Attempts to open another learner's record are refused and written to the audit log.
  • Grades appear on a portal only after the school releases them. Discipline and guidance details are not shown to students.
  • Portal sessions sign out automatically after a period of inactivity (15 minutes by default) — useful on shared devices.

8. Security

  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins.
  • Every role sees only the screens and actions it has been granted.
  • Every change to a record is audited with the old and new values. Grades lock after the encoding window, and changing them afterwards takes two approvers.
  • Downloaded files use short-lived signed links.

Because the data lives on the school's equipment, the school is responsible for protecting that computer and network: Windows account security, disk encryption where appropriate, firewall settings, physical access, and keeping backups safe. We recommend keeping the Host on the local network unless remote access is properly secured.

9. Retention and deletion

The school decides how long to keep records, in line with DepEd, CHED and other legal requirements. Records the app "deletes" are first marked deleted, so the action can be audited. The school can remove data permanently from the database or its backups.

The Go Live feature removes the bundled sample school (a fictional school with made-up people) and its demo logins. Backups copied off the Host — to a USB drive, another PC or a cloud folder — are not touched by the app or by uninstalling it, so the school must delete those itself.

10. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, people whose data is in the app have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Because the school controls the records, send these requests to the school. The app helps the school respond: it can export records to Excel, correct records (with an audit trail) and produce transcripts and permanent records.

11. This website

This website is a static information page. It sets no cookies, has no sign-in or forms, and contains no analytics or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested. Links to the Microsoft Store and other sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes — for example, a new feature that sends data outside the school — we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before that version is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
