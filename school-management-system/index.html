Grades & Assessment

Grades that can't be quietly changed

  • Teachers encode written work, performance tasks and quarterly assessments in one matrix; the term grade is computed and transmuted as they type
  • DepEd K-12 weights per learning area, or the college 1.0–5.0 scale
  • Encoding happens only inside the window. After it closes, grades lock
  • A change after the lock needs the chair and then the registrar — two different people — and the audit log keeps the old and new values
  • LMS quiz and assignment scores land in the matrix with no re-encoding
Cashier & Billing

A cashier window the auditor will like

  • Find a learner by student no., LRN, name or applicant no. and see the live statement
  • Payments go to the oldest installment first; the OR prints with the amount in words
  • OR numbers come from a controlled series — a void keeps its number and needs a reason
  • End of day: counted cash against posted ORs, with any gap in the series listed
  • Transcripts, diplomas and transfer credentials are released only when the learner is CLEAR

Questions

Good to know

Does it follow DepEd grading?

Yes. Written work, performance tasks and quarterly assessment are weighted per learning area following DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015, and the initial grade is transmuted with the official table. College programs use the 1.0–5.0 scale. The weights and tables are data you can adjust, not code.

Can it handle both K-12 and college in one school?

Yes. Kinder, elementary, junior and senior high use homeroom sections and quarters; college uses programs, sections per program, semesters, per-period attendance, schedule choice with seat caps and unit-load rules — all in the same database.

How do parents get access?

The registrar creates a portal account from the guardian record and verifies the link to each child. Parents then sign in from any browser. Grades appear only when released — on the release calendar date or when the report card is claimed.

Does it send text messages?

Absence alerts, announcements, installment reminders and substitute notices go into an SMS outbox. Connect your SMS gateway in School Setup → Settings and they are delivered; without one, the outbox records what would have been sent.

Can we try it before entering our own data?

Yes. Load the sample school — Sampaguita Valley Academy — and sign in as any of the 16 demo accounts. When you're ready, Go Live removes the sample data, keeps the reference tables and restarts every number series.

Can we get our data out?

Yes. Every list — learners, guardians, grades, payments, anything — exports to Excel with its current filters, and a full backup of the database can be downloaded from inside the app at any time.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs on your school network. The internet only matters if you want parents to reach the portal from home, or to deliver SMS through a gateway.

Where is our data stored?

In one SQLite database file on the Host machine — rrd_school_management.db — plus a folder for uploaded files. It is never sent anywhere. Back it up, restore it or move it to another PC from inside the app; a nightly backup runs automatically.

We used RRD Suite Fixed Assets. What happens to our data?

It is carried over. On first start the old data folder and database file are renamed to the school system's names automatically, and the asset register lives on as the Fixed Assets workspace.
