1. Introduction

RamRam Dev ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the RRD Suite: Manufacturing ERP desktop application distributed through the Microsoft Store.

Our application is specifically engineered as a 100% offline desktop tool. By design, we do not operate remote servers, cloud databases, or tracking scripts that gather your personal or business operational records.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because the RRD Suite: Manufacturing ERP runs entirely on your local Windows PC or local company network, we do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any of the following data:

  • Personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or Microsoft account details).
  • Your business records, customer lists, supplier directories, inventory counts, or financial sales reports.
  • Telemetry data, usage analytics, clickstream data, or automated crash logs.

Your business data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly choose to export or share it yourself.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide its core functionality, the application creates and manages data locally on your storage drive. This includes:

  • Database Files: Local SQLite or relational database files storing inventory items, purchase orders, sales receipts, and staff user roles.
  • App Preferences: Local configuration files maintaining user interface settings, language selection (English/Arabic), and window dimensions.

You maintain total ownership and sole custody of this database file. You can back it up, move it across network computers, or delete it at any time without our intervention.

4. Security of Your Information

Because all data is stored locally on your device or secure local network, the security of your business records depends on the physical and digital security measures of your computer system (such as operating system passwords, disk encryption, and local user permissions). We strongly recommend maintaining regular local backups of your application database.

5. Third-Party Services

The application is distributed via the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect standard download statistics and licensing validations in accordance with the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytical trackers, or external cloud APIs inside the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is a professional business and manufacturing management tool intended for factories, workshops and other commercial manufacturing businesses. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements or statutory requirements. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the application following any modifications constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
