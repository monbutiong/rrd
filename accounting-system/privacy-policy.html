Privacy Policy

RRD Suite: Accounting System — by RamRam Dev

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Applies to the Windows desktop application distributed through the Microsoft Store

The short version: RRD Suite has no cloud account, no vendor database and no telemetry. Your accounting data lives in one SQLite file on your own computer and is never sent to us. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the RRD Suite: Accounting System desktop application ("the app") published by RamRam Dev ("we", "us") on the Microsoft Store. It does not cover websites, products or services operated by other companies.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not require registration, does not create an account, and does not transmit personal data to us. Specifically:

  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking libraries are embedded in the app
  • The app does not "phone home" — once installed, it runs fully offline
  • We do not receive copies of your books, your payroll data, or any of your files
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information — we never hold any

3. Where your accounting data lives

All company records — general ledger, payables, receivables, payroll, inventory, tax filings, audit trails — are stored in one SQLite database file on your own disk, plus an uploads folder for receipts and documents you attach. Backups are written where you choose, on your own machine or network.

The app communicates only over your own local network (Host + Client mode or browser access). Because we have no servers involved in running your books, we cannot access, retrieve or restore your data for you — keep your own backups using the built-in backup and restore tools.

4. Microsoft Store purchases

The app is sold exclusively through the Microsoft Store. Payments, licensing checks and refund requests are handled by Microsoft under Microsoft's Privacy Statement. We do not receive your payment details. We may see anonymised aggregate sales data (e.g. number of licences) through the Store's developer reporting.

5. Information you choose to send us

If you contact us for support — for example by e-mail or through the Store listing — we receive whatever you choose to include (your name, e-mail address, and message). We use it only to answer your request, and we do not add it to any marketing list. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your issue.

Please do not send us your database file or screenshots containing real payroll or customer data unless we ask for them while helping you debug a problem — and then only what is necessary.

6. Permissions the app uses

The app uses standard Windows capabilities only where needed to function:

  • Local network access — so other staff PCs can connect to the Host in multi-user mode
  • File system — to read and write its database file, uploads folder and your backups
  • Printing — to print cheques, invoices, BIR/GCC forms and reports

7. Compliance by design

Because your data never leaves your control, you remain the data controller for any personal data of your employees, customers and suppliers processed in the app (relevant to the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, and similar GCC legislation). The app supports your compliance with features such as field-level audit trails, role-based access and segregation of duties.

8. Children

RRD Suite is professional business software intended for accounting use. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data — for example, if an optional cloud feature is introduced — we will update this page and the effective date above, and disclose the change in the Store listing before it takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Reach us through the Microsoft Store listing or via the support contact shown there.
