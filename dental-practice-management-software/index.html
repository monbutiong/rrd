Questions

Good to know

Is it real dental practice software, or a spreadsheet with a UI?

It runs the full life cycle per patient — registration, booking, check-in, examination on the teeth map, treatment plan and approval, treatment session by session, invoice and receipt, then follow-up and recall. Every field change is stamped with the user and the time, and tooth charting keeps its full history.

How do supplies and stock work?

Consumables carry batches and expiry dates. Each treatment has default materials, deducted automatically when a session is completed — earliest expiry first, expired batches never used. Low-stock and expiring items raise alerts, and purchase orders with receiving post delivered stock into the right batch.

Can it handle billing, insurance and partial payments?

Yes. Completed sessions appear on the invoice once, at the current price. Record cash, card, transfer, e-wallet or insurance payments in part, track the balance and the insurer's share, issue numbered receipts, and void only before any payment. Collections roll up into the daily revenue report.

Can several people use it at the same time?

Yes. Staff PCs connect to the Host over your clinic network — the desktop app or a browser. One server process owns the single database and runs every query in order, so there is never a write conflict. A clinic of dozens of users is well within range.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs on your own network. The only time the internet matters is confirming the Microsoft Store licence occasionally; after that it runs offline for months at a time.

Where is my data stored?

In one SQLite database file on the Host machine's disk, plus an uploads folder for photos and documents. It is never sent anywhere. You can back it up, restore it, or move it to another PC from inside the app.

How is it licensed?

It's a paid Microsoft Store app with a 30-day free trial. The licence is tied to your Microsoft account; a copied install won't run on another machine. One purchase covers one server — staff PCs connect to it and need nothing of their own.

Can we brand it with our clinic?

Yes. Upload your logo, set your clinic details and pick theme colours — it flows through the app and the printed reports.
