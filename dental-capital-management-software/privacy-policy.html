Privacy Policy

RRD Suite: Dental Practice Management Software · Effective date: September 20, 2026

In short: RRD Suite runs entirely on your own computers and network. Your patient, staff and financial records are stored in a database file on your own server. The developer does not collect, receive, transmit or have access to any of it, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

1. Who we are

RRD Suite: Dental Practice Management Software (the “App”) is published by Ramram Dev (“we”, “us”) and distributed through the Microsoft Store. This policy explains how the App treats information when you install and use it.

Because the App is self-hosted software, the clinic or organization that installs it (“you”, “the Organization”) decides what data is entered, who can see it, and how long it is kept. You act as the controller of that data; we are the software publisher only.

2. Data the App handles

Depending on which modules you use, the App lets your staff record and manage:

  • Patient information — demographics, contact details, identifiers, emergency contacts, allergies, medical and dental history, appointments and visits, teeth-map charting and treatment plans, consultations, vitals, prescriptions, X-rays and photos, and uploaded patient documents.
  • Staff information — employee records, positions and departments, rosters and attendance, payroll and payslips, and login accounts with role permissions.
  • Operational and financial records — invoices, payments and receipts, follow-up and recall records, clinic supply stock, purchase orders and receiving, equipment, and an activity/audit log.

All of this is stored in a single SQLite database file, plus an uploads folder for photos and documents, on the computer that hosts the App (by default under %APPDATA%\RRDDentalPractice). It is stored on your hardware, under your control.

3. What we collect

We do not collect any of the data described above. Specifically:

  • No patient, staff or financial data is sent to us or to any server we operate. We do not operate a cloud service for the App.
  • The App contains no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting service, advertising SDK or tracking of any kind. Error logs are written to a file on your own machine and are only seen by your administrators.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we never receive it.
  • We cannot access, recover or reset your data. Backups and restores are performed by your administrators from inside the App.

4. Microsoft Store licensing

The App is a paid Microsoft Store product with a free trial. To confirm that an installation is licensed or in trial, the App asks Windows for the Store licence state for your Microsoft account. That check is performed by Windows and Microsoft, and is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We receive only what Microsoft reports to publishers about purchases and downloads through Partner Center.

To let a licensed installation keep working during internet outages, the App stores a small signed licence receipt on the host machine. It is bound to that machine and contains no patient or staff information.

5. Security

  • User passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • Access is controlled by roles and per-page permissions (view, add, edit, delete, approve and similar actions) set by your administrators, so staff only see the modules their role allows.
  • Significant actions are recorded in an activity log stamped with the user and time.
  • Optional browser access and API tokens are turned on and scoped by your administrators; we recommend keeping the App on a private clinic network rather than the public internet.

No system is perfectly secure, and the security of the stored data ultimately depends on how the host computer, network and user accounts are protected. See the next section.

6. Your responsibilities as the Organization

Health and personal data are regulated in most countries. Depending on where you operate, this may include laws such as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Philippines), HIPAA (United States), the GDPR (European Union / UK), or local equivalents. Complying with them is your responsibility, including:

  • having a lawful basis and any required patient consent for the data you record;
  • giving patients and staff their own privacy notices and honouring their access, correction and deletion rights;
  • restricting who can log in, using strong passwords, and removing accounts for people who leave;
  • protecting the host machine, network and backups (disk encryption, firewalls, restricted physical access);
  • keeping regular backups in a secure location; and
  • reporting any data breach to regulators and affected individuals where the law requires it.

The App is a record-keeping tool. It is not a substitute for clinical judgment or professional legal advice.

7. Retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your own server, you decide how long it is kept. Records can be deleted or archived from inside the App, and an administrator can remove sample/demo data using the Go Live function. Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete the database and uploads folder in your user data directory — delete those files yourself if you need the data permanently removed. Copies in backups remain until you delete the backup files.

8. Third parties

The App does not embed third-party services that receive your data. The only external party involved is Microsoft, through the Microsoft Store and its licensing system, as described in section 4. If your administrators enable optional features that connect to outside systems (for example, an API integration or e-mail sending that you configure), data is sent only to the destinations you set up, under your own control.

9. Children

The App is business software for healthcare organizations and is not directed at children. Pediatric patient records may be entered by authorized staff as part of normal care; those records are handled entirely under the Organization’s own policies and applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised, and material changes will be reflected here. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or the App’s privacy practices can be sent to the publisher, Ramram Dev, through the support options on the App’s Microsoft Store listing.

For requests about a specific patient’s or employee’s record, please contact the clinic or organization that operates your instance of the App — we do not hold that data and cannot act on it.
